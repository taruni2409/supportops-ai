# NovaBank Bank Transfer Policy

## 1. Pending Transfer

A bank transfer may remain pending while payment checks are completed.

Customers should allow up to two business days for a standard pending transfer
to complete.

If the transfer remains pending for more than two business days, support agents
should escalate the case to the Transfers team.

## 2. Failed Transfer

A failed transfer means that the payment could not be completed.

Support agents should verify that the recipient details are correct and confirm
that sufficient funds were available when the transfer was attempted.

Customers may retry the transfer after confirming the information.

Repeated failures should be escalated to the Transfers team.

## 3. Transfer Not Received

If the sender's account shows a transfer as completed but the recipient has not
received the money, the customer should verify the recipient account details.

The recipient should also check with their own bank.

If the transfer remains missing after the expected processing period, support
agents should open a transfer investigation.

## 4. Cancelling a Transfer

Completed bank transfers cannot normally be cancelled automatically.

If a transfer is still pending, support agents may check whether cancellation
is available.

If the transfer has already completed, the customer should contact the recipient
and the Transfers team should be consulted where appropriate.
