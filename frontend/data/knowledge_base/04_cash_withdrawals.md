# NovaBank Cash Withdrawal Policy

## 1. Cash Withdrawal Declined

If a customer's cash withdrawal is declined, support agents should first confirm
that the card is active and has not been frozen.

The customer should verify that sufficient funds are available and that the ATM
supports the card.

If withdrawals continue to be declined at multiple ATMs despite sufficient funds
and an active card, the issue should be escalated to the Cash Operations team.

## 2. Cash Not Received

If an ATM transaction is shown as completed but the customer did not receive
cash, the customer should not immediately repeat the same withdrawal.

Support agents should record the ATM location, withdrawal date, withdrawal
amount, and the last four digits of the card.

The case should be submitted for a cash-withdrawal investigation.

Cash-withdrawal investigations should normally be reviewed within seven
business days.

## 3. Incorrect Cash Amount

If the ATM dispenses an amount different from the amount recorded on the
customer's account, the customer should report the discrepancy immediately.

Support agents should collect the ATM location, transaction amount, actual
cash received, and transaction date.

The case should be escalated to the Cash Operations team for investigation.

## 4. ATM Fees

Some ATM operators may charge their own withdrawal fees.

NovaBank support agents should distinguish between a fee charged by NovaBank
and a fee charged by the ATM operator.

If the origin of the fee cannot be determined from the transaction details,
the case should be escalated for further review.
