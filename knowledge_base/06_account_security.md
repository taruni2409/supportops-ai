# NovaBank Account Security Policy

## 1. Suspected Card Compromise

If a customer believes their card details have been compromised, the affected
card should be frozen as soon as possible.

Support agents should review recent transactions with the customer without
requesting sensitive authentication information such as the full PIN.

Any unauthorised transactions should be handled through the appropriate fraud
investigation process.

## 2. Lost or Stolen Card

A lost or stolen card should be frozen immediately to prevent further use.

The customer should confirm whether any recent transactions are unrecognised.

If suspicious transactions are identified, the case should be escalated to the
Fraud and Security team.

A replacement card may be requested after the affected card has been secured.

## 3. Suspicious Account Activity

If a customer reports activity they do not recognise, support agents should
determine whether the activity involves card payments, transfers, withdrawals,
or account-access attempts.

The customer should be advised to secure their account credentials.

Cases involving suspected unauthorised access should be escalated to the Fraud
and Security team.

## 4. Lost Phone or Device

If a customer loses a device that was used to access NovaBank, support agents
should advise the customer to secure access to the account.

The customer should change relevant account credentials where appropriate.

If there is evidence that another person accessed the account, the case should
be escalated immediately to the Fraud and Security team.
