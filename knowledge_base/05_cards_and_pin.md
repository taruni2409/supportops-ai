# NovaBank Cards and PIN Policy

## 1. Card Activation

New or replacement cards must be activated before normal use.

Customers should complete card activation through the NovaBank application or
another supported activation method.

If activation repeatedly fails, support agents should verify the card status
and escalate the issue to the Cards team.

## 2. PIN Blocked

A PIN may become blocked after repeated incorrect attempts.

Customers should not continue entering possible PIN combinations once the PIN
has been blocked.

Support agents should verify the customer's identity before providing available
PIN recovery instructions.

If the PIN cannot be restored using the supported recovery process, the case
should be escalated to the Cards team.

## 3. Changing a PIN

Customers may change their PIN using an available supported PIN-management
method.

Support agents should never ask customers to provide their full PIN.

If the PIN-change option is unavailable or repeatedly fails, support agents
should verify the card status and escalate the issue to the Cards team.

## 4. Damaged or Unusable Card

If a physical card is damaged and can no longer be used reliably, support
agents should confirm that the customer's account remains accessible.

A replacement card may be requested through the supported card-replacement
process.

If the damaged card is also suspected to be compromised, the security procedure
should be followed before replacement.
